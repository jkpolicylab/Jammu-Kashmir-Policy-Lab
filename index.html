About JKPL

Jammu Kashmir Policy Lab (JKPL) is an independent research and policy initiative committed to producing evidence-based research, encouraging informed public dialogue and developing practical policy solutions. JKPL focuses on the institutional, political and social challenges shaping Jammu & Kashmir and seeks to contribute to a more informed, accountable and effective policy environment.

Research Areas

Our research agenda focuses on issues where evidence, institutions and public policy intersect.

Governance

Research on public institutions, accountability, representation, service delivery and governance challenges.

Institutional Reform

Examining institutional structures and developing practical approaches to improve their effectiveness.

Political Mobilization

Studying political movements, public participation, collective action and changing political dynamics.

Conflict Transformation

Exploring pathways for dialogue, peacebuilding, social cohesion and constructive transformation of conflict.

Public Policy

Translating research and evidence into practical policy recommendations for decision-makers and citizens.

Society & Public Dialogue

Creating spaces for evidence-based discussion on important political, social and institutional questions.

Publications & Insights

JKPL will publish policy briefs, research papers, reports, opinion pieces and other evidence-based contributions.
