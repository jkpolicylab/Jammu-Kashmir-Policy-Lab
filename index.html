Our Purpose

Turning evidence into ideas for better institutions and better policy.

JKPL seeks to contribute independent research, constructive analysis and informed public debate on the political, institutional and social challenges facing Jammu and Kashmir.

About JKPL

Research that connects evidence with public policy.

Jammu Kashmir Policy Lab is being developed as an independent research and policy institute focused on evidence-based inquiry, institutional development and constructive public dialogue.

JKPL examines policy and governance questions through research, analysis and engagement with citizens, institutions, researchers and other stakeholders.

Our work is intended to move beyond slogans and assumptions by asking practical questions, examining available evidence and developing policy-oriented recommendations.

The Lab will gradually develop research publications, policy briefs, field studies, opinion analysis and public policy discussions.

“Let evidence prevail over assumption, and informed dialogue over polarization.”

Our Approach

Independent. Evidence-based. Constructive.

JKPL aims to create space for serious research and informed discussion across difficult policy questions.

Evidence

We prioritize verifiable information, research and transparent reasoning in developing policy analysis.

Independence

We seek to maintain an independent research perspective and distinguish analysis from political advocacy.

Dialogue

We believe difficult questions require space for disagreement, evidence and constructive public conversation.
